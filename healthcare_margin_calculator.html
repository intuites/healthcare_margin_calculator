Taxable vs. non-taxable pay. The W2 hourly wage is taxable, so burden (employer FICA, FUTA/SUTA, workers' comp, insurance, etc.) is applied only to the W2 wage. Travel stipends (lodging + M&IE) are generally non-taxable reimbursements only if the clinician maintains a qualifying tax home under IRS rules (duplicated living expenses, regular work/residence ties to the tax home, assignment temporary — generally under 1 year), and they're paid under an accountable plan. Stipends exceeding GSA rates, or paid to workers without a valid tax home, may be taxable wages (and then subject to burden). Admin/VMS fee is treated as a cost line against the gross billing rate. This calculator is an estimate, not tax advice — confirm with your tax/payroll advisor.
